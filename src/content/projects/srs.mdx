## Context

The Student Record System (SRS) gives Universitas Terbuka staff modules for registration, tutorials (TTM), graduation and certification.

## My part

I work on the staff-facing modules:

- **Registration** — staff-side course registration.
- **Tutorials (TTM)** — face-to-face tutorial administration.
- **Graduation** — the staff side of the graduation flow.
- **Certification** — certification records.
