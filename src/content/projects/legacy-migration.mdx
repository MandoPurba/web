## Context

Universitas Terbuka's legacy Laravel services were migrated to NestJS and Go within my first year at the Directorate of Digital Technology (DTD).

## Approach

I moved the services one piece at a time rather than rewriting everything at once: each slice was migrated on its own, so the legacy Laravel code and the new NestJS and Go services ran side by side while the move was in progress.

## Result

The migration finished inside my first year at DTD.
