## Context

Universitas Terbuka's course registration and exam seat booking lets students reserve their own exam seat by location, date and session, and has handled 857K+ registrations.

It runs on the national university platform serving 800K+ students, built at the Directorate of Digital Technology (DTD).

## Problem

Exam seats are limited per location, date and session, and many students book at the same time. Two concurrent requests must never claim the same seat, and seats held by registrations whose hold expires must return to the pool.

## Approach

- **Atomic seat claiming.** A seat is claimed with `FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED`: each transaction locks one free seat row and skips rows already locked by other transactions, so concurrent requests take different seats instead of colliding.
- **2-hour holds.** A claimed seat is held for two hours.
- **Expired-seat reclaim.** When a hold expires, the seat is reclaimed based on the registration's billing status.

## Key decisions & trade-offs

- **`SKIP LOCKED` instead of plain `FOR UPDATE`.** Plain `FOR UPDATE` makes concurrent requests wait on the same locked row; `SKIP LOCKED` lets each move on to the next free seat. The trade-off is that seat assignment under contention is not strictly ordered.
